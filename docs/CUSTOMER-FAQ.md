# Nerix Global customer FAQ catalog

These approved preset answers are shared by the connected chat API and the standalone website widget. They require no OpenAI requests. Edit `shared/faq.ts` and run `npm run build` to regenerate the public widget and this catalog. Free-form Site settings knowledge controls AI responses; it does not rewrite preset answers.

## What does Nerix Global do?

Nerix Global is building AI voice and website-chat services for businesses. Alex focuses on friendly lead generation and follow-ups; Sarah focuses on reception and customer support. We welcome early-access enquiries and agree each service's supported features before activation.

## What does Alex handle?

Alex is Nerix Global's outreach assistant for introducing an approved offer, checking interest and capturing useful contact details. His style is friendly, smart and relaxed, with no aggressive selling or guaranteed results. Live calling and telephone routing still need service activation.

## What does Sarah handle?

Sarah is Nerix Global's support assistant for answering approved questions and helping visitors reach the right person. Website FAQ help, human chat and saved follow-ups are available when the chat backend is connected. Voice reception, bookings and telephone transfers need their own agreed activation.

## Can you provide chat services for my website?

Yes, website chat can be arranged alongside voice services. A connected widget can offer approved help, collect a reason and contact details, and let an available team member join. Each client needs its own approved content, website connection, staffing and privacy arrangements; chat pricing is agreed separately.

## What is the difference between basic and advanced support?

Basic support focuses on routine reception and approved questions. Advanced support may involve more complex routing or supported business-system connections. We define and test the actual workflow with you; advanced support does not mean every CRM or booking system is already connected.

## Can businesses in Pakistan, the UK and the US use your services?

We welcome business enquiries from Pakistan, the UK, the US and other countries. Service availability depends on the required workflow, languages, telephone destinations and provider permissions. Tell our team where you operate so the supported setup can be confirmed before an agreement.

## Can I choose the agent name, voice, language and tone?

Agent names, conversation styles, preferred voices, language preferences and approved business instructions can be configured separately. Alex's outreach style and Sarah's calm support style are independent. Live language and voice performance must be tested for your setup; the preset FAQ helper uses prepared replies rather than freely translating conversations.

## Is the full calling platform live today?

The platform is in development and welcomes early-access enquiries. The dashboard, FAQ/human chat and saved workflows can be explored, but this release does not place real telephone calls or take live card payments. We confirm the accepted voice, carrier, calendar and payment connections before a paid calling service is activated.

## How do I get started with Nerix Global?

Use “Talk to our team” or email info@nerixglobal.com with your business, country, preferred service and expected usage. We review your needs, explain the supported setup and agree rates and any separate charges. A demo registration or chat enquiry does not activate live calls or charge your card.

## What information do you need to set up my service?

Prepare your business name, website, approved offer or FAQs, target countries and languages, opening hours and team contact destinations. For calling, discuss your authorized prospect list, telephone provider and any calendar or CRM connection. Do not put passwords, API keys, card details or identity-document scans into chat or lead notes.

## Can I try a demonstration before paying?

Ask the team for a guided demonstration of the available workflows. Simulated calls do not dial real numbers, and demonstration credit has no cash or refund value. We confirm the scope of any live trial separately; this chat does not grant free paid calling minutes.

## What are your starting voice service rates?

Voice service starting rates are $0.30 per billable minute for outbound, $0.50 for basic reception/support and $0.75 for advanced support. Your agreed industry or customer rate is confirmed before activation. Telephone numbers, carrier usage, taxes and separately scoped connections may add costs; chat pricing is separate.

## How much does website chat cost?

Website-chat pricing is agreed separately based on the chosen FAQ or AI mode, expected usage, human staffing and setup. The $0.30/$0.50/$0.75 voice-minute rates do not automatically price text chat. Preset FAQ replies do not use OpenAI, while enabled AI replies can incur API usage costs.

## Is there a setup fee or a monthly AI package?

Nerix's stated service plan has no setup fee and uses prepaid, pay-as-you-go AI service credit. It is not an unlimited calling package. Recurring number rental, carrier services and any separately agreed integration costs are disclosed in your quote. Other businesses' own products may have different terms.

## What is the minimum first deposit and later top-up?

The first deposit and every normal later top-up must be at least USD100. You can add more; the service wallet and base prices use USD. Payment is only made through an approved method after your supported service and terms are confirmed. This FAQ does not create a payment or credit your account.

## How much AI time does $100 buy?

At the starting AI service rates, $100 corresponds to about 333 minutes 20 seconds outbound, 200 minutes basic support, or 133 minutes 20 seconds advanced support. This is a rate calculation, not a promised number of successful calls. Separate carrier, number and other agreed costs are excluded.

## When does billable AI time start?

The intended calling plan bills connected AI time by the second, beginning after the call is answered and the AI attaches. Ringing-only time is not billed as AI conversation time. Listening and connected IVR handling can count while the AI remains attached; a separately agreed carrier may have its own billing rules. Current dashboard calls are labeled simulations.

## What happens when my balance runs low or reaches zero?

The prepaid plan is designed to stop new paid calling when available credit is exhausted. Your dashboard shows credit and usage; active reservations also affect availability. Live cutoff enforcement is part of carrier integration acceptance. Automatic card recharge is not active in this release.

## Can I pay by Visa, Mastercard or Safepay?

The planned card checkout uses Safepay, subject to merchant approval and tested integration. Live card checkout is not active in this release, so we cannot promise that a particular bank or card is accepted yet. Our team confirms approved payment instructions before payment. Never send card numbers, CVV or banking passwords through chat.

## Which currencies can I select?

Base service pricing and wallet credit use USD. The planned initial checkout choices are PKR, USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, AED and SAR, subject to supported merchant approval. Preview conversion rates are illustrative, not live market quotes; the actual currency amount and applicable costs must be confirmed before paying.

## Can I pay by bank transfer or wire?

A bank or wire transfer may be used only when the Nerix team supplies and approves instructions for your payment. Credit is recorded after cleared funds are verified, not from a transfer screenshot. Your bank or an intermediary may charge separately. Do not use unverified account details or assume another company's receiving account is suitable.

## Will my bank charge extra fees?

Your card-issuing bank may add its own foreign-currency fees, exchange-rate spread or taxes. These are separate from Nerix's merchant processing costs and the service credit advertised in an approved quote. We do not promise a fee-free bank statement or add an undisclosed surcharge; ask our team to confirm the final payment terms.

## Can my balance recharge automatically?

Automatic recharge is planned as an optional feature with a chosen threshold, recharge amount, monthly cap and explicit customer authorization. Saving preferences does not activate a charge. The provider authorization and saved-payment integration are not active in this release.

## Can I get a refund of unused paid credit?

Our proposed policy returns approved unused paid service credit through the original payment method and charged currency, without deducting Nerix's merchant processing costs from that credit. Consumed service time and demonstration credit are not unused paid funds. Requests are reviewed and reconciled first; your bank's fees or conversion may affect its statement. Confirm the applicable published terms before payment.

## Can I upload my own leads?

The dashboard supports CSV/XLSX lead imports with a preview and column mapping. Supply valid country-code phone numbers, the recipient timezone and approved contact information. Use your own authorized list; the example leads are fictional and must not be dialed. This service does not automatically supply a verified prospect database.

## How do calling hours, retries and opt-outs work?

Campaign rules use the recipient's timezone, allowed days/hours, attempt limits and retry spacing. Recorded opt-outs are excluded from future campaign calls. You must agree an authorized outreach approach and any relevant local requirements before live calling is activated; this FAQ is not permission to call every number.

## Can Alex follow up and arrange callbacks or meetings?

Lead history, dispositions, callbacks and meeting requests can be recorded in the dashboard. A stored request is not a confirmed external calendar booking or an automatic telephone callback. Supported calendar connections, staff availability and actual calling are tested and activated separately.

## How will warm transfers to my team work?

The planned warm-transfer flow captures the caller's name and reason, briefs the staff member privately, and connects only after acceptance. The AI leaves after the bridge is confirmed. If staff decline or are unavailable, a callback can be recorded. Real telephone transfers still require the carrier and transfer integration to be accepted for your setup.

## Can I keep my current number and caller ID?

We check number forwarding, supported SIP/carrier arrangements, authorized caller ID and any new number rental with you. Keeping an existing public number may be possible through a supported setup, but compatibility and destination costs must be confirmed. Numbers and transfer legs can have charges separate from the AI service rate.

## Can you connect my CRM, dialer or booking system?

Discuss the exact provider and workflow with our team. We assess its supported API/SIP options, access requirements and separate integration scope; we do not claim universal compatibility. External calendar, CRM, PMS and live carrier connections are not automatically activated by creating an account or saving a profile.

## How many calls can run at the same time?

Call capacity depends on the accepted carrier channels, voice integration and safe billing limits. We start with controlled testing and agree tested concurrency before increasing it. The current simulation workflow is limited; we do not promise unlimited calls, a fixed daily volume or guaranteed sales.

## Can I manage multiple locations and team members?

Workspaces support companies, locations and individual team invitations. An owner can manage access; staff and managers see the areas permitted by their role and assignment. Customers have separate private workspaces. Invite people through their own accounts rather than sharing the owner's login.

Choose “Talk to our team”. Give the reason first, then your name, email, phone with country code and contact permission. An available team member can accept the chat; if nobody joins, a connected chat saves a follow-up. You can keep using preset help while waiting.

Human availability depends on the team shown in the connected chat. If nobody joins, your agreed contact details, reason and conversation are saved for follow-up; staff contact you manually. Use the Support area in your dashboard or request our team in chat, explaining the problem and the email associated with your account. ## How do you handle my chat details and privacy?

A connected chat stores messages and consented contact details for support and follow-up by authorized staff. Preset FAQ mode does not send questions to OpenAI; enabled AI replies use the configured provider. Follow the Privacy link for the website notice and contact info@nerixglobal.com about access, correction or deletion. Do not send sensitive records; we do not claim automatic HIPAA/GDPR certification or active call recording.

## Am I chatting with AI, preset help or a person?

The chat labels its current mode. Preset FAQ help uses prepared replies without OpenAI. If AI is enabled, it generates replies from approved knowledge; when a person joins, the chat identifies that person and automatic replies pause. A disconnected widget cannot save a team request and clearly offers an unsent email draft instead.

We do not guarantee sales, appointments, Google indexing or search positions. Outcomes depend on the offer, audience, approved workflow and other factors. The website describes planned and available services; our team confirms your actual service scope and expected measurements before activation.
