A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we talk.

Have a different question?
Email our team

Can I offer AI and human chat on my website?

Yes. We can set up website chat with your approved business knowledge, a helpful AI first response, and a human handoff. Visitors give their reason before requesting a person. If nobody joins, the request and agreed contact details stay in the team follow-up queue. Chat pricing and activation are agreed separately. Use the round logo button to ask about your setup.

Is Nerix Global available to use today?

We're welcoming early-access enquiries while the platform is being built. Contact us to discuss your requirements and service availability. This website does not create an account, take payments or launch calls.

Can my agent use a different name, voice or language?

That's part of your setup. We plan the agent's name, supported voice, languages and business instructions with you, then test the selected combination before activation.

Will every company use the same workflow?

No. Your workflow is based on your business. A sales team may need qualification and live transfers; a reception team may need questions, bookings and callbacks. We agree what is supported for your setup.

Can the AI transfer a call to my team?

Warm transfers are part of the planned service. The agent provides the caller's name and a short briefing, connects after staff acceptance, then leaves. If the team is unavailable, the workflow can capture a callback request.

Can I keep my current numbers or CRM?

We'll check compatibility with your carrier and systems. You can discuss bringing your own supported SIP setup, a supported dialer or CRM connection, or a complete Nerix calling arrangement. Some systems need a separately scoped connector.

How will prepaid credit and billing work?

The planned dashboard will show your monetary balance, assigned rate and estimated remaining AI time. Connected agent time is billed by the second, including listening and IVR handling. Service pauses when available credit is exhausted until you top up. Carrier and other agreed charges may be separate.

What determines outbound calling hours?

Your campaign follows approved calling windows in the recipient's timezone. We plan lead eligibility, retry limits and opt-out handling with you before activation. Requests to stop contact are excluded from future campaign calls.
