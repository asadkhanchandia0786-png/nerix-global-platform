NERIX GLOBAL WEBSITE

Website privacy.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This notice covers the Nerix Global marketing website. It covers enquiry drafts, website chat and human-help requests. The separate calling platform has its own activation and service requirements.

Enquiry drafts

The enquiry form prepares a message in your own email application using the details you enter. The website does not submit the form to a server, store its contents in a database, or send the email automatically. You decide whether to send the draft.

If you email us, we receive the information in that message and use it to respond to your enquiry and discuss your requirements. Please do not include confidential customer records in your enquiry.

Website chat and human follow-up

When the chat server is connected, your messages and conversation are stored in the Nerix chat service so the assistant and authorised team members can respond. You may request a person by providing your reason, name, email and phone number and agreeing to contact about that request. These details are used to handle your enquiry and follow up; they are not a subscription to marketing.

If text AI is enabled, chat messages are processed through OpenAI to generate answers. The integration requests disabled response storage, but OpenAI's applicable processing and retention terms still apply. The separate contact form fields are not automatically added to the AI conversation. Please do not put passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or confidential customer information into chat.

Prepared FAQ help is clearly identified and does not require AI processing. It also remains available when AI is disabled or unavailable. If the chat server is disconnected, FAQ replies run locally and human-help details only prepare an email draft. That draft is not sent or saved to the team inbox automatically.

Saved chat transcripts and requests are retained for support and follow-up. Contact info@nerixglobal.com to ask about access, correction or deletion. Before enabling this service for another business, the permitted staff access and that business's privacy/retention arrangements must be agreed.

The usage calculator

The pricing calculator runs in your browser. It does not open an account, add credit or send your input to a backend service.

Cookies and analytics

The chat widget uses session storage in your browser tab to keep an opaque chat-session token so a page refresh can resume the conversation. It does not store the contact form fields there. This website's code does not include advertising pixels or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to serve the website. Any additional hosting analytics should be reflected in this notice before they are enabled.

Phone and email links

Telephone and email links open the relevant application on your device. Any message you send or call you make is handled by your chosen provider and the receiving service. Following a link does not start an AI demonstration on this website.

Future services

The calling platform is in development. Details about call records, retention, account data and service privacy will be provided for the applicable service before it is activated.

Contact

For questions about this website or information you have sent us, email info@nerixglobal.com.
